# I.D: Healthcare equipment, supplies, and environments

## Domain: Concepts in Healthcare as Applied to Simulation (10%)

### [Status]: 🟢 Studied

### [Technician Perspective]
**Healthcare Equipment, Supplies, and Environments:** Distinguish between ICU, ER, Med-Surg, OR, and L&D environments. Each requires different monitor layouts, equipment (Ventilators, Defibrillators, Crash Carts, IV Pumps, Glucometers), and supplies. Fidelity is enhanced by using real clinical equipment when safe.

### [Clinical Application]
The environment (Physical Fidelity) significantly affects student stress and immersion. A messy, chaotic ER room is more realistic for an emergency scenario than a clean, quiet classroom.

### [Key Terminology]
*   **Crash Cart:** A tray or wheeler with equipment for resuscitation.
*   **Defibrillator:** A device that gives an electric shock to the heart to restore a normal rhythm.
*   **Fidelity:** The degree to which a simulation mimics reality (Physical, Conceptual, Psychological).
*   **IV Pump (Infusion Pump):** A device that delivers fluids and medications in controlled amounts; a common environment-specific prop that should be programmed to match the scenario, not just present as a static shell.
*   **Staged Clues:** Physical items placed in the environment (e.g., an empty pill bottle, a medic alert bracelet) to provide diagnostic information to the learner.

### [Exam Scenario]
**Q:** You are setting up a 'Med-Surg' room for a stable patient. Which equipment is UNNECESSARY?
**A:** A ventilator and a fully stocked crash cart (these should be nearby but not necessarily in the room, to maintain environmental realism).
