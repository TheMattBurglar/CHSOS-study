Chapter 5: Building Your Dream Team

Hiring for a simulation center is tricky because the perfect candidate doesn't usually come from one single background. 

When looking for a Technology Specialist, you want someone with a "MacGyver" spirit. They need to understand computer networking and A/V systems, but they also need to be comfortable in a clinical setting. They might come from an IT background, but they could also be a former E-M-T or a theater technician who knows how to use props and lighting.

For Educators, you need people who are comfortable moving from "expert" to "facilitator." In simulation, the teacher doesn't just lecture; they guide the students to find the answers themselves during the debriefing.

The chapter emphasizes that "attitude is as important as aptitude." Because simulation technology is always changing, you need a team that is curious, flexible, and willing to learn on the fly. Cross-training is vital—the tech should know a bit about the medicine, and the educator should know how to turn on the manikin. This overlap ensures that if one person is out, the training doesn't stop.
