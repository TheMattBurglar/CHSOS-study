# V.C.1: Needs assessment

## Domain: Concepts in Instructional Design as Applied to Simulation (15%)

### [Status]: 🟢 Studied

### [Technician Perspective]
**Needs Assessment:** Identify the 'Gap' between current performance and desired performance. Specialists provide data on what equipment or modalities can best bridge that gap (e.g., 'We need more code blue training because our actual hospital response times are slow').

### [Clinical Application]
Needs assessment ensures simulation resources are targeted at the areas of highest clinical risk or lowest learner confidence.

### [Key Terminology]
*   **Gap Analysis:** Comparing documented current performance (e.g., incident reports, skills checkoffs, chart audits) against a desired benchmark to define exactly what the simulation must address.
*   **Stakeholders:** Those who identify or fund the need (e.g., hospital administrators, risk management/quality departments, nurse educators, accrediting bodies).
*   **INACSL Standards of Best Practice: SimulationSM (Design):** The published standard requiring a documented, systematic needs assessment before scenario design begins; common data sources include root-cause/gap analysis, SWOT analysis, stakeholder surveys, and outcome data from prior sessions.

### [Exam Scenario]
**[Analysis Level] Q:** A hospital's quality department reports a rise in medication errors, but a separate nursing survey says the real complaint is poor teamwork during code events. The SimOps specialist has limited budget for one new scenario this quarter. What should drive the decision?
**A:** Triangulate the two data sources rather than picking one: check whether the incident reports and survey responses point to the same root cause (e.g., communication breakdowns during high-acuity medication administration). A needs assessment should reconcile 'hard' outcome data with 'soft' stakeholder-reported needs before committing resources, since chasing only one source risks building a scenario that doesn't actually close the real gap.
