Chapter 20: The "MacGyver" Guide to Sim

This is the "Tips and Tricks" chapter. It's packed with practical hacks for the H-S-T-S. 

Moulage Hacks: You'll learn how to make realistic-looking blood, bruises, and even bone fractures using common household items like syrup, food coloring, and gelatin. 

Manikin Maintenance: The chapter gives advice on how to extend the life of your equipment. For example, using a specific type of cleaner to prevent manikin skin from staining, or how to "patch" a puncture wound in a silicone arm. 

DIY Training Aids: Sometimes, the market doesn't sell what you need. This chapter shows you how to build your own task trainers—like using a plastic pipe and some foam to create a "needle decompression" model. Being a tech specialist means being a part-time engineer and part-time artist.
