# IV.F: Professional development opportunities

## Domain: Professional Role: Behavior, Capabilities, and Leadership (15%)

### [Status]: 🟢 Studied

### [Technician Perspective]
**Professional Development & Exam Knowledge:** Stay current through in-person **conferences** (IMSH, SimGHOSTS) and low-cost **webinars** offered by SSH, SimGHOSTS, and manufacturers; webinars are especially valuable when travel budgets or time are limited, since most run an hour or less and count toward continuing education. Also pursue journal clubs, vendor-led training, and stackable digital badges to round out a professional development portfolio. Understand certification standards: for example, CHSOS uses the 'Angoff Method' for standard setting. The exam has 115 questions, but 15 are unscored 'pre-test' questions used for psychometric validation.

### [Clinical Application]
Understanding psychometrics ensures that when you help faculty design simulation exams, the passing scores are statistically valid and defensible, not just arbitrary numbers.

### [Key Terminology]
*   **Angoff Method:** A standard-setting method where a panel of experts estimates what percentage of minimally competent candidates would get each question right.
*   **Pre-test Questions:** Unscored questions hidden in an exam to gather data for future tests.
*   **Scaled Score:** Transforming a raw score into a standardized scale.
*   **Webinar:** A live or recorded online seminar (e.g., through SSH's Webinar Library or a manufacturer) that offers a low-cost way to earn continuing education credit without travel.

### [Exam Scenario]
**Q:** During a certification exam, a candidate encounters a question covering an experimental, unreleased technology. Why is this likely included?
**A:** It is likely one of the 15 unscored 'pre-test' questions used to gather psychometric data for future exams, and does not affect the candidate's final score.

**Q:** A specialist has no travel budget this year but wants to keep growing professionally. What is the most practical professional development option?
**A:** Enroll in a **webinar** series through SSH or a manufacturer/vendor, which typically costs little or nothing, requires no travel, and still provides continuing education credit and current best-practice content.
