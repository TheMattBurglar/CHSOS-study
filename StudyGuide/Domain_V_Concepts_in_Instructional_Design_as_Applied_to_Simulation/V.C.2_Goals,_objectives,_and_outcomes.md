# V.C.2: Goals, objectives, and outcomes

## Domain: Concepts in Instructional Design as Applied to Simulation (15%)

### [Status]: 🟢 Studied

### [Technician Perspective]
**Goals, Objectives, and Outcomes:** Support the creation of SMART objectives. The specialist must understand these objectives to ensure the 'Tech' supports them (e.g., if the objective is 'Team Communication', the audio recording is more critical than the manikin's lung sounds).

### [Clinical Application]
Objectives define the 'Success Criteria' for the learner. If the tech fails to capture the data needed to measure these objectives, the simulation is ineffective.

### [Key Terminology]
*   **SMART Objectives:** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
*   **Learning Outcome:** What the learner is expected to know or be able to do at the end of the session.
*   **Bloom's Taxonomy:** Classifies objectives into three domains: **cognitive** (knowledge), **psychomotor** (skills), and **affective** (attitudes/communication). Each domain has verbs ranging from lower-order (remember, understand) to higher-order (apply, analyze, evaluate, create); the domain and verb tell the specialist which technology actually needs to work.

### [Exam Scenario]
**[Analysis Level] Q:** Two objectives for the same code-blue scenario are: (1) 'Learners will correctly place electrode pads within 30 seconds' and (2) 'Learners will demonstrate closed-loop communication during the code.' Which Bloom's domain does each target, and how should that change what the specialist prioritizes technically?
**A:** Objective 1 is **psychomotor** — the priority is a reliably functioning task trainer/manikin with accurate haptic feedback and a timer or software log that can capture the 30-second benchmark. Objective 2 is **affective/behavioral** (communication) — the manikin's physical fidelity matters less here than high-quality audio capture, since the debrief evidence for this objective comes from the recording, not the vitals log.

### [Notes & Lab Application]
*   Ask yourself: 'What is the one thing the learner MUST do today?' and make sure that tech works 100%.
