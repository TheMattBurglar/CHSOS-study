Chapter 15: The Business of Sim

Running a simulation center is like running a small business. Even if you aren't the director, the tech specialist is often the one who tracks the "consumables"—all the fake blood, gloves, and syringes that the center uses every day.

One of your most important tools is the Inventory Management System. You need to know exactly what you have on the shelves so that you don't run out of supplies in the middle of a big training week. 

You also help track Center Utilization. This means counting "Contact Hours"—how many students used the center and for how long. This data is what the center uses to justify its budget to the university or hospital. 

Finally, the chapter covers Policies and Procedures. You need clear rules for everything: from how to clean the manikins to who is allowed in the control room. These rules keep the center running smoothly and ensure that everyone is treated fairly.
