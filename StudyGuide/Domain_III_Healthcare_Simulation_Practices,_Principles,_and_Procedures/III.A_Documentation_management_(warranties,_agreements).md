# III.A: Documentation management (warranties, agreements)

## Domain: Healthcare Simulation Practices, Principles, and Procedures (25%)

### [Status]: 🟢 Studied

### [Technician Perspective]
**Documentation & Equipment Management:** Maintain a centralized repository for manuals, warranties, and maintenance agreements. A **Warranty** is included with purchase and covers manufacturing defects at no cost, typically for 1 year. A **Maintenance Agreement (Service Contract)** is a separate, often paid, agreement purchased to extend coverage beyond the warranty period, bundling scheduled PM visits, parts, and labor; know which one applies before calling a vendor about a broken part, since out-of-warranty repairs without a service contract are billed per incident. Distinguish between Preventive Maintenance (PM - scheduled) and Corrective Maintenance (repair after failure). Document all repairs to ensure warranty validity and compliance with **SSH Accreditation Core Standards**.

### [Clinical Application]
Safety & Compliance: If a simulator has a recurring electrical fault or pneumatic leak, it must be 'Red Tagged' and removed from clinical use until repaired and validated. Accurate logs prevent equipment failure during high-stakes exams.

### [Key Terminology]
*   **PM (Preventive Maintenance):** Scheduled upkeep.
*   **Corrective Maintenance:** Fixing equipment after a failure.
*   **SSH Accreditation:** Formal recognition that a sim program meets the Society for Simulation in Healthcare's rigorous standards.
*   **Warranty:** Manufacturer-backed, no-cost coverage for defects or failures for a defined period after purchase; can be voided by undocumented repairs, unauthorized parts, or damage from non-approved moulage/cleaning products.
*   **Maintenance Agreement (Service Contract):** A separate, typically annual, purchased agreement that extends vendor-provided PM visits, parts, and labor coverage after the original warranty expires.
*   **Asset Tag:** A label used to identify and track a physical piece of equipment, tying it to its own warranty/service record in the equipment log.

### [Exam Scenario]
**Q:** A simulator's warranty is about to expire and the center has no maintenance agreement in place for next year, nor internal maintenance logs for the past year. Why is this a problem, and what should the operator recommend?
**A:** Without documented maintenance, warranty claims may already be at risk, and once the warranty lapses any repair becomes a billed, per-incident cost. The operator should recommend the center evaluate purchasing a **Maintenance Agreement** before the warranty ends and begin logging PM/corrective work immediately, since **SSH Accreditation** bodies also require documentation of equipment up-time and safety checks.
